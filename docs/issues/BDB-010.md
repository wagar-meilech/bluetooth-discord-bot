# Implement trusted live-stream controls, recovery, and idle policy

Parent spec: https://github.com/wagar-meilech/bluetooth-discord-bot/issues/2
Local ID: BDB-010
Depends on: [BDB-008 (#6)](https://github.com/wagar-meilech/bluetooth-discord-bot/issues/6)

## Purpose and scope

After the complete hardware gate passes, complete the agreed session behavior on the reusable Discord/voice stack. Keep one explicit source and destination, with no track library or player remote control.

## Acceptance criteria

- Owner plus explicit trusted user/server policy, relevant voice context, and caller-channel join.
- Join/leave/status/source/volume/pause/resume commands; only Bluetooth or locally approved host app can be selected remotely.
- No unapproved phone takeover, stale-frame backlog, mixed inputs or concurrent destinations.
- Source switch/pause/disconnect clears queued audio; phone/player playback stays under source-user control.
- Retry only prior selected source/active destination for transient loss, at most 30 seconds, then clear failure status and explicit retry.
- Leave/Stop/Quit immediately cancel forwarding and recovery.
- Leave after 60 seconds alone, without automatic rejoin.
- Diagnostics exclude audio, recordings and tokens.

## Test seam and validation

Approved bridge-session public control/input seam, with only OS/Discord boundaries substituted, in red/green vertical slices. Real encrypted voice recovery/member changes are checked at the installed-system seam.
